I've finally said goodbye to *Zenless Zone Zero*. After playing for so long, my enthusiasm has gradually faded; the game's formula keeps repeating the same few tricks, and I've slowly grown tired of it, losing the fun I felt at the beginning. The miHoYo formula may be a classic, but after playing it so much it really does get a bit stale… And lately I've had less and less free time—a busy life makes me want to save my time for the things that truly help me relax.

I'll leave a few gacha screenshots here as a little memento of this journey. Looking at these images, I can almost recall that tiny thrill of excitement when I pulled them.
