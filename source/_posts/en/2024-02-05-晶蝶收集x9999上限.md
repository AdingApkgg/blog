After a long time, I've finally collected 9,999 Crystalflies in Genshin Impact! That's the per-item storage cap for a full inventory—pretty neat, huh..

As a casual player who's long since run out of things to do in this game, at some point I had a thought: "Collect Crystalflies until I hit the inventory cap."

I don't clear every spot each time—I just farm a few fixed locations, like: `风起地、晨曦酒庄、孤云阁、层岩巨渊` etc..

One run nets me around 40-something, and after subtracting daily expenses that leaves 30-something.. Then I repeat this every day, and over time I slowly crept toward the cap.

![](/img/202402050050250.webp)

Haha, assuming I use 5 a day, I won't have to worry about running out of Crystalflies for the next 2,000-plus days. Awesome!
