## Preface

Let me write down my second-ever experience with a **lucid dream** and **dream control** that I had a while back..

As for the first **lucid dream**, it happened many years ago. From what I vaguely remember, I didn't succeed at **dream control** that time, and I've mostly forgotten the details anyway, so I'll let it go and not dwell on it. No record for that one..

## Before Sleep

I ** jerked off** once while showering. After I got out, I watched some **long videos** about **a passenger plane crashing after a mid-air disaster** and **getting ripped off buying a knockoff phone** for a while, then went to sleep..

## Posture

My right side was lying on the `丛雨` dakimakura of `1.8m`..

## Mindset

Years in advance, I had laid down a **mental imprint** of wanting to spend my life with a certain someone who is close to my **ideal girl** in my mind..

## The Process

I don't remember **her face** or exactly **what she looked like**, but I have a rough impression of the **clothes** she was wearing,

and during the process of disconnecting from the **dream world**, **the brain's cleanup mechanism** caused me to lose that part of the memory..

But I clearly remember that while **in the dream**, I knew what **clothes** she was wearing..

I remember that during the dream I also successfully **controlled** one action in the dream,

That's right, **dream control**!

I remember trying several times to **issue commands of control** to myself in the dream,

and after trying for a while, I finally **forcibly intervened** and made my dream self hug her,

and I also vaguely remember that she was a **flat chest (small breasts)**!

That real feeling of bodily **contact**, which **modern technology** can't compare to, was just so awesome!

According to what I still remember, after the hug ended, she took my hand and **floated up out of thin air**,

and then, with the front of my belly facing the ground, we started **spinning** in a **circular motion**, slowly accelerating. The **feeling of weightlessness and acceleration** grew stronger and stronger,

and as we spun, the connection gradually **broke**, and eventually I **exited** the dream world..

## After Waking

For a short while after waking up, I felt an unprecedented **excitement**, the kind of feeling you get when you're going through a **major life event**,

and once I realized I was awake, I sat on the bed and **thought** for a little while..

It occurred to me that if I didn't **write this down in my notes**, then after some time this precious experience would probably **be lost forever**..

By then I'd really have missed out! At that thought, I picked up the phone I'd left by the bed, opened my notes app, and started **temporarily** recording the specific details..

After a while, I **organized** the memories my brain had at the time, **cross-validated each detail in the dream**, and **proofread** the notes I'd jotted down temporarily,

and that's how this **article** came to be published..

Because this sleep was **very short**—I **woke up** in less than an hour—I didn't experience much **stuff** **in the dream**, which is a real shame..
