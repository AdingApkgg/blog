After waiting the better part of a year, Hu Tao's rerun is finally here!

I never touched a banner during that whole stretch, so I'd banked over 200 pulls!

Goal: C1 Hu Tao plus one Staff of Homa. Let's go!

## How the Pulls Went

### Hu Tao Rerun Character Banner

First, 10 pulls — one Hu Tao secured..

![202307080604483](/img/202307080604483.webp)

20 pulls! I lost the 50/50, but it still counts for something — one Jean..

![202307080604486](/img/202307080604486.webp)

60 pulls! C1 Hu Tao achieved! Total cost: 60 Intertwined Fates. On to the weapon banner!

![202307080604484](/img/202307080604484.webp)

### Staff of Homa Rerun Weapon Banner

![202307080604488](/img/202307080604488.webp)

30 pulls! Lost to the mint spear..expected, honestly.

![202307080604489](/img/202307080604489.webp)

90 pulls! Staff of Homa dropped! `目标达成`, on to Yelan's banner.

![202307080604490](/img/202307080604490.webp)

### Yelan Rerun Character Banner

![202307080604491](/img/202307080604491.webp)

80 pulls on Yelan's banner! Rough luck — lost to Keqing.. And the few dozen Intertwined Fates I picked up with Masterless Starglitter and Masterless Stardust didn't bring her home either. Ugh..

![202307080604482](/img/202307080604482.webp)

Still, my luck early on was so good that I'd already hit my goals, so it's no big deal — just a little disappointing.
