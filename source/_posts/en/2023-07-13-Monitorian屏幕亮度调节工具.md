Most monitors require you to press buttons on the back to adjust brightness, and since it's easy to forget which button controls brightness, you often end up hitting the wrong one—such a hassle!

So I went to the world's largest sex community platform and found this [Monitorian](https://github.com/emoacht/Monitorian) brightness-adjustment utility to replace the buttons. It makes adjusting brightness much more convenient, and the interface is somewhat similar to laptop brightness controls.

![Monitorian](/img/202307131718127.webp)

[Microsoft Store 版本](https://www.microsoft.com/store/apps/9nw33j738bl0) offers more settings and features, such as hotkey support.

## Requirements

- Windows 7 or later
- [.NET Framework 4.8](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/zh-cn/download/dotnet-framework) runtime
- DDC/CI must be enabled on the monitor; it's usually enabled by default on modern displays
