From `2009` all the way to the present `2024`, Minecraft `Minecraft` has stood the test of a long, long time — and now it's finally hit its 15th anniversary!

Having freeloaded without a legit copy for quite a while, I saw the ridiculously slashed price this time, and the urge to finally pay up and go legit `Minecraft` suddenly hit me..

## 0x0001

![MC15周年骨折价](/img/202405312341172.webp)

Deluxe edition `59.50`, standard edition `44.50` — the prices are absolutely slashed to the bone. If not now, when?!

## 0x0002

Buying from the official [Minecraft](https://minecraft.net) website ran into country and region restrictions,

![更改国家或地区跳过限制](/img/202405312354058.webp)

After changing the country or region under Microsoft account > Your info > Profile info > Country or region to another country to bypass the restriction, other problems cropped up instead. How strange..

## 0x0003

![购入订单](/img/202406010009163.webp)

After struggling with it for a while to no avail, I had no choice but to buy it from the Microsoft Store using Alipay. The price gap didn't seem like much, so I just went straight for the deluxe edition.
